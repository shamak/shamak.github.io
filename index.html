Shamak Dutta

I am a research scientist in Meta's Applied AI organization.

I was a postdoctoral researcher in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Waterloo where I worked with Yash Pant and Stephen L. Smith on large scale combinatorial optimization problems in flight planning (with Airbus) and data-efficient learning in robotics and control (PhD thesis).
